# 60–90 second demo script

**0–10 seconds — Introduction**

"This is Sush, a voice-enabled Telegram AI shopping and styling assistant I built with n8n. It supports both text and voice messages."

**10–30 seconds — Product search**

Send: "Find running shoes under ₹2,000."

"For shopping requests, the AI agent searches Amazon India through ScraperAPI and returns real product information directly in Telegram."

**30–50 seconds — Voice capability**

Send a short voice note asking for styling help.

"For voice queries, the workflow downloads the Telegram audio file, normalises the filename, and transcribes it using Groq Whisper before it reaches the AI agent."

**50–70 seconds — Styling response**

Show Sush's response.

"The assistant can also provide conversational styling guidance, asking for context and then suggesting outfits, footwear, colours, and accessories."

**70–90 seconds — Workflow proof**

Show the n8n workflow and a successful execution.

"This is the full n8n automation—Telegram input, voice transcription, Gemini AI processing, Amazon retrieval, and an automated Telegram response."
